# Day 7: Create the volunteering questions guide

**Main task:** complete B02 and its question sheet. **Planning effort:** 4-6 hours.

## Target and inputs

**Primary query:** questions to ask before volunteering with children. **Destination:** `/resources/questions-before-volunteering-with-children`.

Read B02 in the [content briefs](../content/content-briefs.md), [keyword intent notes](../research/03-search-landscape-and-serp-notes.md), and the [publishing checklist](../templates/publishing-checklist.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] Recheck whether the query fits local-program preparation in the selected market; avoid accidentally writing an overseas-volunteering guide.
- [ ] Write about role clarity, commitment, training, supervision, boundaries, accessibility, and the provider's screening and concern-reporting process.
- [ ] Create an original question sheet with question, provider answer, and follow-up fields.
- [ ] Attribute general background sources accurately without claiming Agape has adopted their policies.
- [ ] Obtain the safeguarding/program review required by the brief. Record the reviewer and the scope of review truthfully.
- [ ] Check plain language, metadata, source links, mobile layout, and the printable sheet.
- [ ] Add links from Resources and Get Involved, and only link to related guides that actually exist.
- [ ] Publish after review and release checks. Use preparation as the next step until real Agape roles and intake are available.

## Output and finish conditions

One reviewed guide and usable question sheet, with accurate author/reviewer information, sources, metadata, and internal links. Publication remains pending if subject review is missing. No invented legal requirement, safety guarantee, volunteer opening, or placement promise is acceptable.

## If the reviewer is unavailable

Finish the draft, evidence notes, and question sheet, then record the review dependency under R18. Mark the day's production partial and continue Day 8. Keep the unreviewed guide out of public output.
