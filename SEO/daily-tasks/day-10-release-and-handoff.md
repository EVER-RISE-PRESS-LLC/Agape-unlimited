# Day 10: Check the release and complete the handoff

**Main task:** verify the ten-day delivery and leave a clear continuation record. **Planning effort:** 4-6 hours.

## Read first

- Daily progress and result notes from Days 1-9.
- [Publishing checklist](../templates/publishing-checklist.md), [technical acceptance checks](../strategy/07-technical-seo-roadmap.md), [measurement plan](../strategy/09-measurement-and-reporting.md), and [report template](../templates/monthly-report.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] Review every changed page and distinguish published, approved/build-ready, draft, and held content.
- [ ] Check the resource hub, contextual links, related resources, and absence of orphan or unpublished destinations.
- [ ] Verify sitemap, robots, canonicals, metadata, schema, assets, and real 404 behavior in the production build.
- [ ] Run the appropriate build, code, browser, accessibility, mobile, and performance checks for actual changes.
- [ ] Recheck sources, printable tools, reviewer attribution, dates, and permissions for anything ready to publish.
- [ ] Review the local/profile/directory decisions and B05-B12 holds; keep incomplete providers or unverified services out of the release.
- [ ] Prepare a small relevant authority prospect list and optional message drafts based on actual resources. Record them as unsent unless sending has been explicitly authorized.
- [ ] Prepare the exact release summary. Push/deploy when covered by the execution session's authorization; otherwise leave a checked release-ready package with the required next action.
- [ ] After an actual deployment, verify changed public URLs and discovery endpoints. Hand Search Console inspection and sitemap submission to the superadmin under A05; do not perform account setup or submission yourself.
- [ ] Write an initial delivery report showing evidence, available baseline, open issues, and follow-up owners.
- [ ] Assign the first full report after 28 reliable measurement days, maintenance checks, and the later quarter review. Leave those outcomes open until performed.
- [ ] Update the shared checklist, backlog, and ten-day tracker with actual results. Call out the still-deferred contact and giving connections in the handoff.

## Output and finish conditions

Save a release record with changed URLs, commit/deployment where applicable, checks, published guides, held work, data availability, and the next assigned actions. The initial report covers delivery and early observations; it cannot claim a full month of results after ten days.

The ten-day package is complete when the assigned work is delivered and verified, with external dependencies explicitly recorded. If a guide or integration remains unfinished, mark it partial or held and report it honestly. A successfully checked release-ready package must not be called deployed.

## If access, review, or release authorization is unavailable

Finish the local checks, ready changes, report, and specific handoff. Keep the missing release action visible. Do not create fake deployment, inquiry, payment, indexing, traffic, or outreach results to close the tracker.

Deploy only within the execution session's authorization and verify production after any release. Keep later evidence reviews and blocked work open, preserve deferred email/checkout, and update the full tracker and handoff.
