**Main task:** decide which queries and pages deserve work first. **Planning effort:** 3-5 hours.

## Checklist

- [ ] Recheck the initial shortlist in the confirmed market, or record an explicitly provisional English-language check if the market remains unknown.
- [ ] Review the 110 records and maintain the distinction between direct observations, hypotheses, local templates, and exclusions.
- [ ] Select the first 10-15 focus queries and assign one primary destination for each coherent intent. The school-drive checklist initially shares B01's page.
- [ ] Add market-specific volume or organic difficulty only when real data is available; record source, date, and market.
- [ ] Confirm B01-B04 as the initial article assignments, with the tools and reviewer each needs.
- [ ] For B05-B08, record intent, original contribution, missing inputs, reviewer, and a develop-later or hold decision. Keep their full existing briefs.
- [ ] Assess local keyword and Business Profile eligibility requirements from actual operations; do not create an ineligible profile or invent an address.
- [ ] For B07, identify the verification and maintenance requirements for a directory; if geography is known, prepare a small source list without claiming a complete directory.
- [ ] Save a page/keyword map and a decision for all twelve briefs. B09-B12 receive their operational decision on Day 3.

## Output and finish conditions

Update the keyword map and save the shortlist and content decisions in the Day 2 result note. Every selected phrase must have a purpose, owner URL, evidence label, priority, and relevant publication condition. Geographic placeholders remain research variables.

This day completes prioritization and research decisions. Writing the four later resource guides is separate follow-up work, with a proposed production slot and dependency recorded for each.
