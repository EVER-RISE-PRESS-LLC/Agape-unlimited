# Day 8: Create the support-without-money guide

**Main task:** complete B03 and its time-and-skills worksheet. **Planning effort:** 3-5 hours.

## Target and inputs

**Primary query hypothesis:** ways to support families in your community without donating money. **Destination:** `/resources/support-families-without-donating-money`.

Read B03 in the [content briefs](../content/content-briefs.md) and the [publishing checklist](../templates/publishing-checklist.md). Validate the exact phrasing rather than treating the generated long-tail query as measured demand.

## Checklist

- [ ] Write a practical guide organized by the time, skills, and support a person can realistically offer.
- [ ] Start with confirming needs and consent; distinguish suggestions from actual available roles.
- [ ] Create an original table for time available, relevant skill, task to ask about, support needed, and boundaries.
- [ ] Use organization-supported examples; avoid presenting unvetted childcare, transport, or professional advice as easy informal help.
- [ ] Check that the guide respects family privacy and does not assume what a family needs.
- [ ] Obtain the factual/editorial review and check readability, metadata, and sources.
- [ ] Build and test the HTML/print worksheet and link from Resources and Get Involved.
- [ ] Connect to the supply-drive and volunteering guides only where those pages are already published.
- [ ] Publish after checks or save the finished draft with the specific remaining dependency.

## Output and finish conditions

One useful guide with a reusable time-and-skills worksheet, accurate examples, unique metadata, sources where used, and links. The page must be useful while online giving is unavailable, without implying that Agape has current openings for each suggested task.

## If local examples or an editor are unavailable

Use clearly conditional general examples and finish the original tool. Keep any unapproved claims out. Record the missing review rather than inventing a role or testimonial.
