# Foundation facts and content intake

The authoritative open-question list is the root [Items for your review](../../NEEDS-YOUR-REVIEW.md). Use this worksheet to gather approved answers and source records; do not maintain a competing list of unanswered questions here.

## Organization and geography

Map answers to R01-R04, R08-R09, R15, and R17. Record the approved public name, legal identity where applicable, service country and area, actual audience, responsible leadership, approved biography, and the public domain decision. Keep a source and date for each answer.

## Resource interview

Gather the actual task, the common point of confusion, what the organization can responsibly explain, a useful tool or example, limits, and the appropriate reviewer. If the topic is outside the person's experience, record that and choose a qualified source or narrower topic.

For a story, record whether the person has approved their identification, quotation, photograph, and the context of publication. Keep sensitive consent records in the foundation's appropriate private system; this repository should contain only the publication status and permitted public information.

## Contact and giving handoff

Map status to R05-R07. Record that the configuration has been approved, who owns it, and which private mechanism stores the credentials. Never paste API keys or private financial details into the worksheet. A checked configuration item also needs a real delivery or checkout verification before public claims are changed.

## Recording answers

Update the stable item in the root checklist with the answer, source, date, and consequences for the plan. Then revise affected keyword gates, page briefs, and schedule. Silence or a generated suggestion is not a confirmed fact.
