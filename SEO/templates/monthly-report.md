## Content decisions

For each important page, state the task, what the evidence shows, and whether to improve, retain, merge, or defer it. Name the specific missing answer or practical obstacle rather than saying a page needs more content.

## Operational or technical issues

Record discovery, canonical, source, availability, form, checkout, or provider-link problems. Name the affected URL, the evidence, impact, owner, and proposed action. Do not claim a technical deployment has already changed Google rankings.

## Next period

List a small number of concrete priorities with owner, dependency, acceptance check, and review date. Link outstanding decisions to [the review checklist](../../NEEDS-YOUR-REVIEW.md). Do not turn an unanswered question into an assumption of approval.

## Client summary

Write five to eight plain-language numbered points: what was completed, what changed in useful visibility, what readers did, what remains deferred, and what happens next. Avoid internal scoring jargon and unsupported ranking promises.
