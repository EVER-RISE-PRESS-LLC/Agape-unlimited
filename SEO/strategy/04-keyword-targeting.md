# Keyword targeting and validation

Use one useful destination for a coherent search intent. The [110-row keyword inventory](../research/keywords.csv) is a research and prioritization tool, not a request to publish 110 pages. Several phrases should be answered by the same page, and fourteen entries are later-stage or excluded terms.

## What the research establishes

The current evidence supports three choices: distinguish the full foundation name, test a small number of task-focused resource topics, and postpone local service targeting until the facts support it. The inventory explicitly separates nine directly researched phrases from generated variants, geographic templates, and exclusions. Qualitative priority is not a measured estimate of search demand.

Monthly search volume, organic difficulty, current rank, expected traffic, and cost per acquisition are unknown. Empty metric fields are intentional. Do not turn them into zeros or use them to calculate a traffic forecast.

The full name alone belongs to `/`. The mission, leadership, and founder variants belong to `/about`. This distinction corrects a common brand cannibalization problem before it appears.

## First twelve terms to validate and develop

### Branded navigation

1. **Agape Unlimited Foundation** — homepage. Use the full organization name in the title, clear organization description, and approved profiles.
2. **Agape Unlimited Foundation mission** — About. Explain the current mission, responsibility, and boundaries.
3. **Agape Unlimited Foundation programs** — program hub. Clarify which areas are planned and which are active.
4. **Agape Unlimited Foundation volunteer** — Get Involved. Keep availability honest until real roles and intake exist.

These do not need a blog post. Demand may be small while the foundation is new; exact identification is still necessary.

### Resource acquisition hypotheses

5. **How to organize a school supply drive** — step-by-step guide with a needs-confirmation worksheet and logistics checklist.
6. **School supply drive checklist** — a prominent section and downloadable or printable tool on that same guide. Split only if there is a distinct user task and enough evidence.
7. **Questions to ask before volunteering with children** — a question sheet reviewed for suitability and safeguarding boundaries. The observed query family includes international volunteering; validate domestic wording for the eventual market.
8. **Ways to support families in your community without donating money** — practical options organized by time, skills, and recipient consent. This exact long-tail demand remains unmeasured.
9. **How small businesses can support local nonprofits** — an original partnership scope worksheet. The related phrase about ways businesses can help was directly researched.
10. **Mentor vs tutor** — a carefully bounded comparison, with a family decision worksheet and references to qualified providers. Verify the exact audience intent before commissioning.

### Local acquisition hypotheses

11. **Youth mentoring volunteer opportunities in `[City]`** — an actual role page, not a generic article. Requires verified location, role, screening process, commitments, and working inquiry delivery.
12. **Family support resources in `[City]`** — a maintained resource-navigation page. Requires a verified local directory and a person who checks changes.

## Evaluation before commissioning

Use this planning score after basic scope checks. It is an internal decision aid, not an SEO platform's keyword-difficulty score.

| Factor | Score | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission fit | 0-3 | 3 means central to an approved audience need |
| Task specificity | 0-3 | 3 means a clear task a page can complete |
| Original contribution | 0-3 | 3 means a useful tool, genuine experience, or verified local detail is available |
| Sustainable maintenance | 0-3 | 3 means the owner can keep the page accurate |
| Suitable next step | 0-3 | 3 means a useful, functioning next step exists |

Maximum 15. A score of 11 or above is a suggested commissioning threshold, not a ranking prediction. Any missing publication gate overrides the score. A local service query with strong mission fit still waits if no active service exists.

Examples:

- A supply-drive guide with an original worksheet and reviewed logistics can score well even before Agape accepts supplies, because the reader can complete a planning task independently.
- A volunteer landing page cannot qualify merely because volunteering matters to the mission. It needs actual roles and a working contact route.
- A generic article about tax-deductible giving fails if the foundation cannot establish its own status and the writer lacks the relevant jurisdictional expertise.

## Metrics validation workflow

### Establish the market

The client confirmed Georgia, United States on 21 October 2026 (R01). Use that service market for future validation. Specific cities/counties, audience priorities, additional languages, and donor markets remain open under R10/R17/R20. English remains the working language. Existing search observations have not been rerun with Georgia localization, and no volume or ranking figures have been added. Keep city placeholders until actual coverage is known; do not substitute Atlanta or infer statewide program availability. Separate service users from donors who may live elsewhere.

### Obtain a keyword export

Import the priority terms into the foundation's available keyword research tool. Record tool, date, country, language, device where supported, date range, search-volume estimate, trend, and difficulty methodology. If using Google Keyword Planner, distinguish its advertiser competition metric from organic difficulty. Its search estimates depend on the selected market and can combine close variants. [Google Ads metric definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575).

No paid account or subscription has been created as part of this plan. A Search Console baseline and focused manual result checks can support initial decisions while a volume tool remains unavailable.

### Review the actual result page

For each shortlisted phrase, note:

- Is the visitor looking to learn, compare, find a local provider, volunteer, or make a gift?
- Are current results guides, directories, program pages, videos, forms, or local listings?
- Are established national sources prevalent, or is there room for verified local or practical detail?
- What information would Agape need to answer the task responsibly?
- Can one existing page serve the intent, or is a new destination warranted?

Do not equate an unfamiliar domain with low competition. Inspect usefulness and fit, not just brand recognition.

### Use actual site data

Once reliable data exists, review page/query groups over a complete 28-day window. Record impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position along with country and device. Search Console does not expose every query; anonymized terms limit exact reconciliation. [Google query data guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12917174).

## Cannibalization rules

| Overlap | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Supply-drive guide versus checklist | One guide and a section first; distinct printable asset linked from it |
| School-supply drive versus backpack drive | Cover the relevant variation in the main guide unless the task becomes materially different |
| Volunteer guide versus volunteer role page | Guide answers preparation questions; role page answers current local participation |
| Mentor versus tutor versus finding a mentor | Comparison explains roles; finder guide explains program selection; do not repeat the same introduction across both |
| Program hub versus individual program | Hub explains the portfolio; individual page needs operational detail not already answered by a short hub entry |
| Multiple city pages | Create only where real service and distinctive local information exist |

When two pages receive impressions for the same query, inspect intent before merging. Overlap alone is not a failure. Merge when the pages answer substantially the same task and maintaining two offers no user benefit; preserve useful unique content and redirect the retired URL.

## Terms to avoid initially

Do not actively pursue urgent aid, rent assistance, guaranteed scholarships, healthcare funding, free vehicles, housing grants, addiction treatment, medical missions, or another Agape organization's name. These searches demand specific capability or identify a different entity. The older brand document does not establish present-day services.

Broad phrases such as `best charities to donate to`, `nonprofit organizations`, and `charity` may have substantial competition and weak organizational fit. They are not a sensible first content commission. `Nonprofit grants for organizations` mainly attracts grant seekers, not grantmakers evaluating Agape.

## Research refresh

Recheck the chosen market and intent before each first article, then refresh the keyword map monthly using actual impressions and reader feedback. Keep rejected queries with the rejection reason so future AI work does not repeatedly propose the same unsuitable topic.
